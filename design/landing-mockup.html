How it works

Five steps from table to plate.

One flow replaces the printed menu, the order pad and the kitchen shout.

01

Scan

A QR at the table opens the menu in the browser — the table number travels with every order.

02

Browse in 3D

Every dish is a model, not a photo. Rotate it, place it on the table, judge the portion.

03

Customise

Spice, jain, no-onion, allergens — captured as structured data, not a scribble.

04

Kitchen

The chef screen receives the ticket instantly and tracks it through preparing → ready.

05

Serve

Waitstaff get the ready alert with the table number. Right dish, right table, first time.
